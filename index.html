How to read a card
  1. A card is a claim (A, T, h). If Maker owns every X and Breaker owns no cell of the drawn region, Maker wins within h more moves.
  2. Maker claims the gray pivot. After that, Maker owns every X on every child board. The blue outline on a child board marks this pivot.
  3. Breaker's reply blocks exactly the children whose envelope contains it. Cells common to every child envelope already belong to Maker, so at least one child survives.
  4. Maker continues with the first surviving child in the certificate's order. Its height is at most h − 1.
